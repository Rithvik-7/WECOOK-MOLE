How to drive

This page only drives. Monitoring and AI stay on the other page.

  • Hold FWD / LEFT / RIGHT / REV, or use W A S D / arrow keys.
  • Release the key or button to stop. Space is an immediate STOP.
  • Watch ultrasonic centimetres and the IR flag separately. IR does not auto-stop.
  • Rover IMU, MQ-7 raw, and vibration are inspection-only. They never enter Node A/B AI.

Connection checklist for tomorrow
